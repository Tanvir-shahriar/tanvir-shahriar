ABOUT

BACK STORY

"I'm a full-stack engineer who gets a little too excited about making complicated things feel simple. I care about the small details, the edge cases everyone forgets, and shipping work that genuinely makes someone's day easier."

I started writing code by taking apart things that already worked just to see why they tick. That curiosity evolved into a career building high-velocity web products, multi-tenant SaaS architectures, and autonomous AI agents.

Rather than chasing fleeting framework trends, I anchor my engineering in timeless principles: clean modular boundaries (Route → Controller → Service → Repository), strict end-to-end TypeScript types, and normalized relational schemas.

In 2024 and 2025, I’ve been focused on bridging the power of LLMs with real-world production constraints — building multi-agent workflows with LangGraph and hybrid semantic retrieval with pgvector.

When I’m not debugging concurrency issues or tuning database index plans with EXPLAIN ANALYZE, you’ll find me exploring stationery shops, tinkering with retro hardware, or drinking good coffee.
